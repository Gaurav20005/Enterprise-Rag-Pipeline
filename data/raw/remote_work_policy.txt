REMOTE WORK POLICY

Document ID: HR-POL-001
Department: Human Resources
Version: 2.1
Effective Date: January 2026

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines the company's requirements for remote and hybrid working arrangements. It is intended to provide employees with flexibility while maintaining productivity, collaboration, information security, and operational continuity.

2. ELIGIBILITY

Employees may request remote working arrangements after completing their probationary period. Approval depends on role requirements, business needs, team availability, and individual performance.

Some roles may require employees to work from an office because of operational, security, customer-facing, or equipment requirements.

3. REMOTE WORK ALLOWANCE

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to three working days per week unless their employment contract specifies a different arrangement.

Employees must coordinate their working schedule with their manager and team.

4. WORKING HOURS

Employees working remotely must remain available during their agreed working hours.

Employees are expected to attend meetings, respond to business communications, and maintain normal service levels while working remotely.

5. INFORMATION SECURITY

Company data must only be accessed through approved devices, applications, and network connections.

Employees must not store confidential company information on personal devices or unauthorised cloud storage services.

Employees must use multi-factor authentication where required.

6. HOME WORKSPACE

Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe and suitable workspace.

The workspace should provide reasonable privacy and prevent unauthorised individuals from viewing confidential company information.

7. EQUIPMENT

The company may provide approved equipment required for remote working.

Employees are responsible for taking reasonable care of company equipment and reporting loss, theft, or damage immediately.

8. MANAGER APPROVAL

Remote working arrangements must be agreed with the employee's line manager.

Managers may decline or modify remote working arrangements where business requirements cannot be met.

9. POLICY REVIEW

This policy is reviewed annually by the Human Resources department.
