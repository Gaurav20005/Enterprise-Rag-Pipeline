EMPLOYEE EXPENSE POLICY

Document ID: FIN-POL-001
Department: Finance
Version: 2.4
Effective Date: January 2026

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines which business expenses employees may claim and the process for submitting reimbursement requests.

2. ELIGIBLE EXPENSES

Employees may claim reasonable business expenses incurred while performing authorised company activities.

Examples include business travel, approved accommodation, public transportation, client meetings, and necessary business supplies.

3. RECEIPTS

Employees must retain receipts for eligible expenses.

Receipts should clearly identify the date, supplier, amount, and nature of the purchase.

4. EXPENSE SUBMISSION

Expense claims should be submitted through the approved expense management system.

Claims should normally be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred.

5. BUSINESS TRAVEL

Employees should use reasonably priced transportation and accommodation.

Premium travel arrangements require prior approval unless there is an exceptional business reason.

6. CLIENT ENTERTAINMENT

Client entertainment must have a legitimate business purpose and comply with applicable company approval requirements.

7. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES

Personal purchases, fines, penalties, unauthorised upgrades, and expenses unrelated to company activities are generally not reimbursable.

8. MANAGER APPROVAL

Expense claims require manager approval before reimbursement.

9. POLICY REVIEW

The Finance department reviews this policy annually.
