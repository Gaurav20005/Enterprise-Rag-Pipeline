INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

Document ID: SEC-POL-001
Department: Information Security
Version: 4.2
Effective Date: February 2026

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes minimum information security requirements for protecting company systems, applications, devices, and information.

2. PASSWORDS

Employees must use strong passwords and must not share passwords with other individuals.

Passwords must not be stored in unapproved documents or communication channels.

3. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION

Multi-factor authentication must be enabled for systems that require it.

Employees must never approve authentication requests that they did not initiate.

4. COMPANY DEVICES

Company devices must use approved security controls and operating system updates.

Employees must report lost or stolen company devices immediately to the IT service desk.

5. DATA CLASSIFICATION

Company information should be handled according to its classification.

Confidential and restricted information must only be shared with authorised individuals.

6. EMAIL SECURITY

Employees must be cautious when opening attachments or clicking links received through email.

Suspicious emails should be reported to the Information Security team.

7. CLOUD STORAGE

Company information may only be stored using approved cloud storage platforms.

Personal cloud storage services must not be used for confidential company information.

8. INCIDENT REPORTING

Security incidents, suspected data breaches, lost devices, and suspicious activity must be reported immediately.

9. ACCESS CONTROL

Employees must only access systems and information required for their job responsibilities.

Access rights are reviewed periodically.

10. POLICY REVIEW

The Information Security department reviews this policy annually.
