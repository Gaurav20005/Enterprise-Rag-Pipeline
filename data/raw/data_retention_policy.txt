DATA RETENTION POLICY

Document ID: DATA-POL-001
Department: Data Governance
Version: 1.8
Effective Date: March 2026

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines requirements for retaining, reviewing, archiving, and deleting company information.

2. DATA RETENTION PRINCIPLE

Information should only be retained for as long as necessary for legitimate business, legal, regulatory, or contractual purposes.

3. CUSTOMER DATA

Customer information must be retained according to applicable legal and contractual requirements.

Teams must not retain customer information indefinitely without a documented business or legal requirement.

4. EMPLOYEE DATA

Employee records should be retained according to Human Resources retention schedules and applicable employment requirements.

5. DOCUMENT REVIEW

Business owners should periodically review stored information to determine whether it remains necessary.

6. DATA DELETION

Information that is no longer required should be securely deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

7. BACKUPS

Backup systems may retain information for a defined period based on the company's backup and disaster recovery procedures.

8. ACCESS

Access to retained information must follow the principle of least privilege.

9. DATA BREACHES

Suspected unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of retained information must be reported through the security incident process.

10. POLICY REVIEW

The Data Governance department reviews this policy annually.
