ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY

Document ID: HR-POL-002
Department: Human Resources
Version: 3.0
Effective Date: January 2026

1. PURPOSE

This policy explains the company's annual leave entitlement and procedures for requesting and managing holiday time.

2. ANNUAL LEAVE ENTITLEMENT

Full-time employees are entitled to 25 days of annual leave per calendar year, excluding public holidays.

Part-time employees receive a pro-rated entitlement based on their contracted working hours.

3. LEAVE REQUESTS

Employees must submit annual leave requests through the company's approved HR system.

Employees should normally submit requests at least two weeks before the intended start date.

4. MANAGER APPROVAL

Annual leave is subject to manager approval.

Managers should consider team capacity, business requirements, existing approved leave, and operational deadlines when reviewing requests.

5. CARRYOVER

Employees may carry forward up to five unused annual leave days into the following calendar year with manager approval.

Additional carryover may be permitted in exceptional circumstances.

6. SICKNESS DURING ANNUAL LEAVE

Where an employee becomes ill during annual leave, the employee should follow the company's sickness reporting procedure.

Relevant medical evidence may be required depending on the length of the absence.

7. CANCELLATION

Approved annual leave may be cancelled where significant business circumstances require the employee's attendance.

The company will provide reasonable notice where cancellation is necessary.

8. POLICY REVIEW

The Human Resources department reviews this policy annually.
